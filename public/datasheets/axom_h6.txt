NOTE ON THE SOURCE GEOMETRY
  This airframe was converted from IGES, which carries no part names at
  all. Every solid arrives as `part_N`, so its documented components were
  identified by position against the six rotor centres rather than by
  name. Solids whose function the geometry does not make obvious are
  deliberately left unnamed in the viewer.
